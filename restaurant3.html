Our Story

Kaifan Mandhi House started with a simple dream of bringing the authentic taste of Arabian cuisine to Kollam. What began as a small restaurant gradually grew into a popular dining destination. Over the years, we have introduced new dishes, improved our services and welcomed thousands of customers. Our passion for delicious food and customer satisfaction continues to inspire us every day.

Our Promise

We believe that great food brings people together. Every dish we serve is prepared with care, quality ingredients and a passion for delivering an unforgettable dining experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the most loved Arabian restaurants in Kerala by maintaining high food quality, introducing new flavours and building lasting relationships with our customers.
